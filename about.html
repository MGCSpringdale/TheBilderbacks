Our story

It started around a piano at home. It still does.

The Bilderbacks are a Southern Gospel family ministry from Springdale, Arkansas. For more than 29 years — since 1997 — Euel and Marsha, together with Blake, Quincie, Cortni, and Caleb, have shared the Gospel of Jesus through music, one gathering at a time. We sing in churches, at revivals, and at festivals — anywhere folks will gather.
